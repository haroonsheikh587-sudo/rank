Why Law Firms Struggle Online

The real reasons clients aren't finding your firm

Legal is one of the most competitive verticals in search. The firms on page one didn't get there by accident — they have a system.

🔒

Google restricts legal ad targeting

Paid ads for legal services face strict policies, rising CPCs, and limited reach. Organic search and local SEO are more sustainable long-term channels for attorneys.

📍

Local competition is brutal

Most law firm searches include city or county modifiers. Winning the Google Map Pack for "personal injury lawyer [city]" requires deliberate local SEO — not just a website.

🤖

AI search is changing how clients find attorneys

Google AI Overviews now answer legal questions directly. Firms that structure their content around real client questions get cited — and get the clicks that follow.

⭐

Reviews and trust are the decision trigger

Legal clients read more reviews than almost any other service. A thin or unmanaged review profile loses cases before you ever get the call.

📄

Thin practice area pages don't rank

Generic "We handle all cases" pages signal nothing to Google. Each practice area needs depth: specific questions answered, local context, and authoritative content.

🐌

Slow, outdated websites hurt conversions

A legal client in crisis will not wait 6 seconds for your homepage to load. Core Web Vitals failures cost you both rankings and the consultation request.
