Real Estate Marketing Challenges

Why most real estate websites generate no leads

Real estate is hyper-local and intensely competitive online. The agents and brokerages winning leads from Google have invested in systems — not just a nice website.

🗺️

"Homes for sale in [neighborhood]" is a goldmine most miss

Hyper-local neighborhood searches have high buying intent and lower competition than city-level terms. Most agents have one generic website and miss all of it.

🐢

IDX-heavy websites are slow and poorly indexed

MLS IDX feeds generate thousands of pages Google often can't properly crawl or index. Without technical SEO, your listings are invisible to search engines regardless of the property count.

📍

GBP profiles are incomplete or unclaimed

Most real estate agents have an unclaimed or half-finished Google Business Profile. This is the fastest local ranking lever available — and most agents aren't using it.

🏆

Zillow and Realtor.com dominate organic

The portals own the generic terms. But neighborhood-specific, agent-expertise, and local market content are where independent agents and boutique brokerages can outrank them.

📝

No neighborhood content strategy

Buyers researching "best neighborhoods in [city]" or "moving to [suburb]" are high-value prospects. Without dedicated neighborhood guides, you're invisible to this audience.

🤝

Referral-only is a growth ceiling

Referral networks are valuable — but finite. Agents who build search visibility generate inbound leads that don't require maintaining every relationship or attending every networking event.
