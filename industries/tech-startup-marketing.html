Why Startups Struggle With SEO

The growth traps most tech startups fall into

Paid acquisition gets you users fast. But it doesn't compound. SEO done right builds a channel that costs less per lead every month — not more.

💸

Over-reliance on paid ads

Google Ads and Meta campaigns can acquire users at scale — but every click costs money. When the budget stops, so does the traffic. Organic search is the only channel that keeps working after you've stopped paying.

🔑

Wrong keyword targeting

Most startups target broad, high-volume terms they can't rank for — "project management software" with zero domain authority. We build a bottom-up keyword strategy that wins real traffic at your current stage.

📝

Blog content that doesn't convert

"10 productivity tips" doesn't bring in SaaS trial signups. We build content that targets high-intent queries at every funnel stage — awareness, consideration, and decision — with CTAs that convert.

🔗

No domain authority

New domains start with zero trust. Without a link building strategy, content stagnates on page 5 regardless of quality. We build editorial links that give your domain the authority needed to rank.

🏗️

Technical debt on the website

Startup websites often have JavaScript rendering issues, slow load times, and poor crawlability — especially sites built quickly on Webflow, Framer, or custom stacks. We fix the foundation before building on top.

🤖

Missing AI search visibility

Google AI Overviews are changing B2B research behavior. Startups whose content gets cited in AI answers get the click. Startups without structured, authoritative content get skipped entirely.
