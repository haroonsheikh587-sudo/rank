Healthcare Marketing Challenges

Why patients aren't finding your practice online

Healthcare is highly competitive in local search. The practices that fill their schedules first have one thing in common: deliberate digital infrastructure.

📍

"Near me" searches are winner-take-all

The top 3 Google Map Pack results capture 70%+ of clicks. Below that, patients almost never scroll. Your GBP ranking is your most valuable digital asset.

⭐

Reviews decide before you do

Healthcare patients read an average of 7 reviews before choosing a provider. A practice with 50 reviews at 4.2 stars loses to a competitor with 200 reviews at 4.8 every time.

📱

Mobile experience is the first impression

83% of healthcare searches happen on mobile. If your site is slow, hard to navigate, or doesn't have a click-to-call button above the fold, patients bounce to your competitor.

🤖

AI Overviews are replacing "best dentist" lists

Google now answers "what to look for in a dentist" with AI-generated summaries. Practices whose content gets cited in these summaries get the patient — the ones below it don't.

🔍

Procedure-specific pages don't exist

A patient searching "Invisalign cost [city]" or "same-day dental crowns" won't find a generic homepage. You need dedicated, optimized pages for every procedure you offer.

🏥

HIPAA-compliant marketing is complicated

Healthcare marketing has compliance requirements that most general agencies ignore. We build campaigns that convert without creating compliance risk for your practice.
