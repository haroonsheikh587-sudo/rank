E-Commerce Growth Challenges

Why most e-commerce stores plateau after launch

Paid ads get you sales fast. But the math breaks down at scale — CAC rises, margins compress, and the moment you stop paying, revenue stops. Organic fixes this.

💸

Paid ad dependency with rising CPCs

Meta and Google CPCs have risen 20–30% year-over-year. Brands 100% dependent on paid acquisition are on a treadmill — running faster just to stay in place as margins erode.

📦

Product and category pages not ranking

Most e-commerce sites have thin, manufacturer-copy product pages that Google ignores. Unique descriptions, structured data, and keyword optimization turn your catalog into a search asset.

🛒

High cart abandonment rate

Average e-commerce cart abandonment is 70%. Most brands accept this. We optimize checkout flows, reduce friction points, and implement recovery sequences that recapture lost revenue.

📊

Google Shopping campaigns wasting budget

Unstructured Shopping campaigns spend heavily on irrelevant queries and branded searches with low incremental value. We restructure to maximize ROAS on the terms that actually convert.

🔍

No content strategy for category pages

Category pages are the highest-value SEO real estate in e-commerce. Without targeted copy, buying guides, and FAQ content, they're invisible to anyone searching above product level.

📱

Mobile checkout is broken

Over 60% of e-commerce traffic is mobile, but mobile conversion rates average 30% lower than desktop. Slow load times, clunky forms, and poor UX on mobile are leaking revenue every day.
