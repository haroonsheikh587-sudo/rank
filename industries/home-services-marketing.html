Home Services Marketing Problems

Why contractors keep paying for leads they should own

Lead aggregators like Angi and HomeAdvisor sell the same lead to 3–5 competitors. You pay $40–$120 per lead and compete on price. There's a better way.

💰

Lead aggregator dependency

Angi, HomeAdvisor, and Thumbtack sell leads to multiple contractors simultaneously. You win on price or lose — and pay either way. Owning your search rankings eliminates the middleman entirely.

📍

Map Pack is winner-take-all

The top 3 Google Map Pack results get 70%+ of clicks for "plumber near me" style searches. Position 4 and beyond barely exists. Getting into the top 3 is the most important thing you can do for your business.

⭐

Competitors have more reviews

In home services, review count and rating are the primary decision factors after ranking. A competitor with 200 reviews at 4.7 stars consistently beats a competitor with 30 reviews at 4.9 — because volume signals trustworthiness.

📱

Website isn't set up for emergency calls

Home service searches often happen in urgent moments — a burst pipe, no heat in winter. If your website doesn't have a prominent click-to-call button above the fold on mobile, you're losing emergency calls every day.

🗺️

No service area page strategy

Contractors serve multiple cities and zip codes, but have one generic website. Without individual service area pages, you're invisible to anyone searching outside your primary city — even if you serve their area.

🔖

Google Business Profile is incomplete

Missing service categories, no photos, no Q&A responses, wrong hours — an incomplete GBP is a ranking penalty hiding in plain sight. It's also the most fixable problem, often showing results within 30 days.
