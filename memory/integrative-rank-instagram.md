## 2. Strategy

- **Audience:** licensed naturopathic doctors (NDs) and practice owners, starting in Portland, Oregon. Patients are not the audience.
- **Why Instagram matters here:** NDs market their own practices on Instagram, and many check a vendor's Instagram before they answer an email.
- **Cadence:** 2 posts a week, Tuesday and Friday, posted 8–10am US Eastern.
- **Plan:** 24 posts from 2026-10-29 to 2027-01-19, in three phases: awareness → trust → action.
- **Content pillars:**

  | Pillar | Posts |
  |---|---|
  | Quick wins | 8 |
  | Founder | 5 |
  | Conversation | 5 |
  | Proof | 4 |
  | Offer | 2 |

- **Graphics only:** no Reels and no video.
- **Built for Instagram:** posts are planned for how people use social media, not reposted blog articles.
- **Daily habit, 15 minutes:** reply to every comment, and leave genuine comments on Oregon NDs' posts. Never pitch in comments or DMs before you have a real relationship.
- **Source of truth for dates and status:** `~/Downloads/integrative-rank/social/Integrative-Rank-Social-Design-Calendar.xlsx`. The working copy is on the Desktop; keep the two in sync.

**Never use:**

- health claims
- location tags

### How a post is made

Each post has its own folder, `social-posts/<date>_<topic>/`, containing:

| File | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `post.html` | The design. Add `?f=ig&s=N` to view each slide. |
| `render.py` | Exports every format at 2x, plus the LinkedIn PDF. |
| `captions.md` | Caption, hashtags, Story steps and LinkedIn text. |
| `output/` | The final PNGs and PDF. |

## 4. Caption rules

- Start with a hook line and one emoji (🌿 is the brand emoji).
- Keep it short and plain. Address NDs directly.
- Links go in the bio ("link in bio"); links in captions don't work.
- End with **one** call to action: Follow, Save, Comment or Apply.
- Use 8–15 hashtags.
- Write alt text under Advanced settings → Accessibility.
- Share every post to your Story, adding a **link sticker** to `integrativerank.com/apply` when it fits.
- Never use these words or claims: skyrocket, 10x, guaranteed, autopilot, "alternative medicine", "specialist/expert", cure claims, fake scarcity.
- Never mention where the team is based.

**Core hashtags:**
`#naturopathicdoctor #naturopathicmedicine #naturopath #integrativemedicine #holistichealth #privatepractice #practicegrowth #healthcaremarketing`

**Add for Portland posts:** `#portlandnaturopath #portlandoregon #localseo`
